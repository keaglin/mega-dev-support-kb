Parser hypothesis in the supplied model

The model describes a shared month-first parser receiving a day-first tenant’s input. Report R-41203 describes 2 December becoming 12 February, which is compatible with that explanation. The report does not supply the original numeric entry or a processing trace that proves it.

Hint hypothesis in the supplied model

The model describes booking-v3 displaying MM/DD/YYYY while its form reads the tenant’s day-first convention. Report R-41241 describes 6 August becoming 8 June, which is compatible with that path if the directory’s form facts applied when the report arrived. The report does not capture the actual hint or entry, and the directory has no form-version history.

These mechanisms can produce the same visible swap. R-41416 and R-41482, from the same tenant, contain identical wording but receive different buckets because their channels differ. The reported date pair alone cannot decide between the model’s explanations.

Received is not the same as merged

The first report was received on 2026-04-01. The first recorded merge was on 2026-07-02, with 184 reports already received; 184 reports were merged that day. The initial queue spike below is a filing event, not evidence that the product first broke in July.

The exported page’s original 30 September review date precedes the latest reports in that export. The current incident page’s review date records our later KB correction, not an earlier product diagnosis. Neither a merge timestamp nor a queued fix proves shipment or recovery.

The desk’s original flags and the body-audit gaps are different evidence sets. A month/day swap does not establish why a report describes a one-day discrepancy or an invite preceding a booking; different local weekdays can also be legitimate views of one instant. These reports need an explicit disposition, not a blanket causal claim.

The concrete counterexamples matter: Alder form versus app has the same subject but different buckets; Copper versus Morrow form traffic has the same subject/channel but different buckets; Pale versus Alder form traffic can have different subjects yet the same hint bucket. An unknown tenant is unclassified. Even equal non-null buckets need corroborated failure evidence before they establish shared causation.

Proposed decision 0002 separates report identity, provisional routing, causal merges and unresolved evidence. It is not adopted and does not silently replace accepted decision 0001. Matching a documented failure mechanism is its proposed basis for causal merges; equal subjects or equal buckets alone are not enough.
