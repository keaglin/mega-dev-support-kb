Parser: day-first input reaches a month-first shared parser

The shared parser reads month first, although the tenant writes day first. For example, report R-41203 describes 2 December becoming 12 February. In the supplied model this is the parser path: 02/12/2026 is intended as 2 December but read as 12 February.

Use the tenant’s date convention or an unambiguous date representation at the parser interface. Check both app/email traffic and the booking form whose hint already agrees with the tenant. A parser change alone does not correct the separate form-hint defect.

Hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to enter month first

The booking-v3 hint prints MM/DD/YYYY; its form reads the value in the tenant’s day-first convention. A person following that hint can therefore submit the opposite date even when the form parser follows the locale. Report R-41241 describes 6 August becoming 8 June: following the hint gives 08/06/2026, which the German tenant’s form reads as 8 June.

Make the hint agree with the form’s actual parsing convention. Keep this ticket separate from the shared parser work: form channel plus booking-v3 is the discriminator, not the browser locale. These examples are scenarios implied by the exported cause model, not executions of product code.
