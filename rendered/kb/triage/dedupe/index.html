This guidance is under review. The #4471 investigation shows that matching subjects can fall into different classifier buckets, and different subjects can fall into the same bucket. The recorded rule below is historical guidance, not proof of shared causation. Decision 0001 remains the accepted record. Decision 0002 proposes separating identity, routing and causal merges; it has not been adopted.

A busy morning brings hundreds of reports about the same thing. We keep one ticket per issue and merge every duplicate into it, so engineering sees one thread with a count.

Recorded rule and rationale from 2024

Decision 0001 recorded the following subject-only rule:

A report is a duplicate when its subject line matches the subject of an open ticket.

  1. lowercase the subject and strip punctuation;
  2. look for an open ticket with the same subject;
  3. found one: merge the report into it. None: open a new ticket.

Matching on subject is fast and needs no reading. During an incident that matters more than anything else.

The review notice qualifies this historical guidance; it does not silently replace the accepted decision.
