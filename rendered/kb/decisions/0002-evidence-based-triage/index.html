0002 · Separate report identity, routing and causal merges

owner Kevon Eaglin proposed reviewed 2026-10-29 · 2 days ago

Date: 29 October 2026 · Proposed by: Kevon Eaglin, with GPT-6.1 Sol

Not adopted. This proposes replacing the subject-only merge criterion in decision 0001 if accepted by the desk’s policy owners. Decision 0001 remains the accepted record while this proposal is reviewed. This draft does not approve a replacement policy, establish a product fix, or authorize closing the #4471 issues.

Context

The desk needs to move quickly without turning convenient grouping into a claim of shared cause. The #4471 investigation exposes three different questions currently being treated as one: whether an export repeats a report, where a report should be investigated, and whether reports describe the same defect.

The classifier is useful executable evidence of its own routing rule, not an independent production reproduction. It uses tenant locale, booking-form label and channel; it does not inspect the reported symptom, original entry, parsed result or timezone. Tenant directory records do not contain historical form versions. Their applicability to an older report must not be silently assumed when it matters.

For example, R-41416 and R-41482 have the same tenant and the same body—“The reminder for 1 November arrived for 11 January”—but different channels put them into different buckets. Conversely, R-41202 describes 12 May versus 11 May while R-41204 describes 10 December versus 12 October; both take the same tenant/channel path and receive the same bucket. Neither comparison by itself proves which reports share a production cause.

Proposed decision

If accepted, the desk would make the following distinctions.

Report identity: count source records, not matching wording

Repeated rows with the same source report ID and identical content count once. Different report IDs remain separate reports even when their subjects or bodies match. Report counts are not automatically counts of distinct bookings, customers or incidents.

Preserve the original IDs and evidence when linking or merging investigations. If records with the same ID conflict, retain and flag that conflict rather than silently choosing one version. The existing export helper is not claimed to implement this proposed conflict-handling rule.

Routing: group work provisionally

Use the supplied classifier, relevant tenant/form/channel facts and the report’s actual wording to choose an investigation route. Record which facts are known, which are snapshot assumptions, and which evidence is missing. Intake tags and browser locale are clues, not substitutes for the tenant’s established input contract.

A routing bucket may collect related-looking reports before their mechanism is confirmed. Label that grouping provisional; do not describe all its members as a diagnosed defect. Unknown or conflicting context remains explicitly unresolved rather than being forced into a confirmed cause.

Causal merges: cite evidence connecting the failure

Subject equality, body equality and classifier-bucket equality are each insufficient on their own. Before merging investigation threads as one defect, record:

  • the expected and observed behavior, with relevant input and tenant/form/channel context;
  • the existing defect reproduction, processing trace or other mechanism-bearing evidence that connects this report’s failure to that defect;
  • any contrary evidence and why the shared explanation still fits.

A report matching a documented reproduction and affected path need not receive a full independent root-cause investigation. A matching date pair alone is not that evidence: an incorrect parser and a misleading form hint can produce the same visible swap.

When that connection is absent, link reports as a provisional investigation group instead of claiming a causal merge. Keep their IDs, counts and differing symptoms visible.

Uncertainty: keep an explicit disposition

Record the unresolved question, a discriminating check and an investigation owner. For example, R-41344 needs the relevant instant and timezone context before differing Lisbon/Toronto weekdays can be called erroneous. R-41236 needs correlated booking and invite timestamps; its wording alone does not identify a date parser failure.

A report without a complete date pair is not thereby a false positive, a new confirmed defect, or proof that an existing defect explains it. Do not erase that uncertainty by changing its bucket.

Closing a product defect requires the evidence appropriate to that work and an explicit disposition for unresolved reports. Investigation completion, a manual date correction, a merged patch, a deployed fix and course acceptance are different outcomes; record which one actually occurred.

Alternatives and trade-off

  • Keep subject-only merging: fast and cheap, but the subject cannot establish identity or mechanism.
  • Treat classifier equality as the merge decision: repeatable, but converts the model’s assumptions into causal certainty and cannot resolve symptoms it does not inspect.
  • Require a complete diagnosis for every report: stronger evidence per report, but unnecessarily repeats work when a documented reproduction and affected path already match.

The proposal keeps fast provisional routing and reuse of established defect evidence, while requiring a recorded basis for a causal merge. It costs more reading and explicit uncertainty tracking than the old rule; it avoids treating that extra work as optional simply because the queue is busy.

Adoption and consequences

This record remains proposed until the policy owners record acceptance. Only then should the desk replace its active merge instructions and link them to this decision. Preserve decision 0001 unchanged as the historical record; this draft does not mark it superseded in advance.

Adoption would require the guide and its examples to distinguish identity, routing and causal merges, and to show what happens to unresolved evidence. The classifier and source report data must not be rewritten merely to make their output agree with a preferred narrative.

No product implementation or policy adoption is included in this draft. The public parser issue #2 and hint issue #1 remain separate tracking; this proposal does not establish that either is fixed.
